A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Curious?
All ears.

More questions? Call or text 760-889-5431. We’d love to hear about your pet.

Do you come to my neighborhood?

Text us your ZIP code and we’ll confirm whether your address is within our mobile service area before arranging an appointment.

How much does grooming cost?

Your quote depends on your pet’s size, coat condition, grooming needs, and location. Send us those details and we’ll discuss the price before you book.

What should I share before booking?

Tell us your pet’s species, breed, size, age, and the service you have in mind. Please mention any sensitivities, handling concerns, or special needs so we can discuss a suitable plan.

How do I book an appointment?

Tap “Text to book” to open your messaging app, or call 760-889-5431. An appointment is confirmed only after we agree on a date, time, and service with you.

What if I need to reschedule?

Call or text us as soon as you can. We’ll discuss availability and confirm any changes directly with you.
